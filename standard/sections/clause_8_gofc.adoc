== Cataloguing considerations

In addition to documenting and describing WIGOS metadata holdings, WMDR observing facility metadata will be searchable via the WIGOS Global Observing Facility Catalogue (GOFC).  As part of discovery and search workflow, a GOFC may choose to apply filters to help narrow search results.  This is realized by the API of the GOFC, as well as applying facets in the catalogue.

=== Faceting

The API of the GOFC, in addition to allowing for spatial/temporal/attribute queries and filters,
may choose to apply facets to WMDRs.  Facets are effective when applied against controlled
vocabularies and classifications.
